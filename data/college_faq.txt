SECTION 1: ABOUT THE COLLEGE
-----------------------------------------------------------

Q: What is Apex Institute of Technology & Management?
A: Apex Institute of Technology & Management (AITM) is a premier engineering and management institution established in 2005. It is affiliated with a State Technical University and approved by AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education). The campus spans 50 acres and is located on the outskirts of the city with excellent transport connectivity.

Q: Is the college NAAC accredited?
A: Yes, AITM has been accredited by NAAC (National Assessment and Accreditation Council) with a B++ grade. The college also has NBA accreditation for its Computer Science, Electronics, and Mechanical Engineering programs.

Q: What is the college's ranking?
A: AITM is ranked among the top 200 engineering colleges in India by NIRF (National Institutional Ranking Framework). It holds a state-level ranking of top 10 in the private engineering college category.

Q: What are the college's working hours and holidays?
A: The college operates Monday to Saturday from 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM. National holidays, Diwali, Holi, and other major festivals are observed. The academic calendar has two semesters: July–November (Odd Semester) and December–April (Even Semester).

SECTION 2: UNDERGRADUATE PROGRAMS (B.TECH)
-----------------------------------------------------------

Q: What B.Tech programs are offered?
A: AITM offers B.Tech (Bachelor of Technology) in the following branches:
   - Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
   - Computer Science and Engineering (AI & ML specialization)
   - Computer Science and Engineering (Data Science specialization)
   - Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
   - Mechanical Engineering (ME)
   - Civil Engineering (CE)
   - Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)
   - Information Technology (IT)

Q: What is the duration of B.Tech?
A: The B.Tech program is a 4-year (8 semesters) full-time program.

Q: What is the intake capacity for B.Tech CSE?
A: The sanctioned intake for B.Tech CSE is 120 seats. B.Tech CSE (AI&ML) and CSE (Data Science) have 60 seats each.

Q: What is the eligibility for B.Tech admission?
A: Candidates must have passed 10+2 (Class XII) from a recognized board with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) as core subjects, with a minimum of 45% aggregate marks (40% for SC/ST candidates). A valid JEE Main score or State Entrance Exam (e.g., MHT-CET, AP EAMCET, KCET, WBJEE) is required.

Q: How do I apply for B.Tech admission?
A: Applications can be submitted online at www.aitm.edu.in/admissions. You need to upload Class X and XII marksheets, entrance exam scorecard, passport-size photo, and ID proof. Counseling is conducted either through centralized state counseling or direct admission based on merit.

Q: What documents are required at the time of admission?
A: Required documents include:
   1. Class X marksheet and certificate
   2. Class XII marksheet and certificate
   3. Entrance exam scorecard (JEE/State exam)
   4. Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC) if applicable
   5. Domicile/Residence certificate
   6. Migration/Transfer certificate from previous institution
   7. Passport-size photographs (6 copies)
   8. Aadhaar card (original and photocopy)
   9. Medical fitness certificate

SECTION 3: POSTGRADUATE PROGRAMS (M.TECH & MBA)
-----------------------------------------------------------

Q: What M.Tech programs are offered?
A: AITM offers M.Tech (Master of Technology) in:
   - Computer Science and Engineering
   - VLSI Design (Electronics)
   - Machine Learning and Data Science
   - Structural Engineering (Civil)
   Duration: 2 years (4 semesters)

Q: What is the eligibility for M.Tech admission?
A: Candidates must hold a B.Tech/B.E. degree in a relevant branch with minimum 55% marks (50% for SC/ST). A valid GATE score is preferred for scholarship benefits. Admission without GATE is also available based on institute-level entrance test.

Q: What MBA programs are offered?
A: AITM's School of Management offers an MBA (Master of Business Administration) with specializations in:
   - Marketing Management
   - Finance and Accounting
   - Human Resource Management
   - Operations Management
   - Business Analytics
   Duration: 2 years (4 semesters), Intake: 60 seats

Q: What is the eligibility for MBA admission?
A: Candidates must have a Bachelor's degree in any discipline with minimum 50% marks. A valid score in CAT / MAT / CMAT / ATMA / XAT / GMAT is required. Group Discussion (GD) and Personal Interview (PI) are also conducted by the institute.

   - Laundry facility
   - Common room with TV
   - Indoor games (table tennis, carrom, chess)
   - Reading room
   - Generator backup for power cuts
   - CCTV surveillance

Q: What is the hostel curfew timing?
A: Hostel curfew is 9:30 PM for all students. Late entry requires permission from the warden and a written request. Outpass for overnight stays requires parental consent.

SECTION 7: EXAMINATION SYSTEM
-----------------------------------------------------------

Q: What is the examination pattern at AITM?
A: The examination pattern consists of:
   - Internal Assessment (CIA): 40 marks
     * Two mid-semester tests (15 marks each)
     * Assignments and attendance (10 marks)
   - End Semester Examination (ESE): 60 marks
   Total: 100 marks per subject

Q: When are the semester examinations conducted?
A: End Semester Examinations (ESE) are conducted:
   - Odd Semester (1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th): November–December
   - Even Semester (2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th): April–May

Q: How do I access my exam results?
A: Results are published on the university website and the student portal (portal.aitm.edu.in). Students receive SMS and email notifications when results are declared.

Q: What is the minimum attendance requirement?
A: Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject to be eligible to appear in the end semester examination. Students with attendance between 65–75% may be condoned with medical or valid reasons. Below 65% attendance leads to detention.

Q: What is the process for re-evaluation of answer sheets?
A: Students can apply for re-totalling or re-evaluation of answer sheets within 15 days of result declaration. The application must be submitted online along with a fee of ₹500 per subject for re-totalling and ₹1,500 per subject for re-evaluation.

Q: What happens if a student fails in a subject?
A: Students who fail in a subject have the following options:
   - KT (Keep Terms) exams: Conducted along with regular exams in the next semester
   - Supplementary exams: For final year students only
   - A student with more than 4 backlogs cannot proceed to the next year
